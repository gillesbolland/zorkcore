How to enter

On an OpenHop Repeater with ZorCore installed: open /plugins/zorcore/, set up the companion, share the meshcore:// link, then DM new game.

Radio tips

  • Long story replies arrive as multipart DMs: 1/3… means more is coming; .3/3 means the story is complete.
  • Exits (and other status tips) are a separate unlabeled message — not part of the n/m count.
  • Lost a chunk? DM ? / hello? to resend the last reply. Use again / g to redo your last command.

Shared dungeon · local fast-pass

Everyone shares one living world — same thief, troll, and cyclops. Remote players queue when the mesh is busy; companions within local_max_hops still get a fast pass.

Reuse / fork

Feel free to reuse this project to adapt other vintage Infocom-style text adventures for MeshCore — same radio chunking, companion DMs, and shared-world patterns. Forks and ports of other classic interactive-fiction titles are welcome.

The MIT license covers the plugin code. Story rights for third-party games stay with their owners; only ship text and assets you have the right to use.
