Safety / mesh gate

Play follows OpenHop Adaptive Rate Limiting tiers (quiet → congested). Remote play stays closed unless ARL is on and the tier is at or below the max you set. Nearby players (≤3 hops) can still get a local fast-pass when remotes are blocked.

Mesh gate

When the gate is on, ZorCore reads /api/advert_rate_limit_stats and only opens remote play after the mesh stays quiet enough for the hold time.

Master switch. Off = legacy multi-session (ban list still applies if Enforce ban list is on). Default: on.

Max play tier

Remote play allowed when ARL current tier is at or below this (quiet / normal / busy). Default: normal.

Queue and locals

Remotes take turns through a FIFO queue. Locals (path ≤ hops, or on the sticky local list) can share the dungeon under the busy-local exception. Idle actives free their slot after the idle timeout.

One remote active player at a time; others wait and get a DM when their turn opens. Default: on.

TX pacing

Half-duplex radios cannot RX and TX at once. These delays reduce lost replies and multipart collisions on the mesh.
